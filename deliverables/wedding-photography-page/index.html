Everything You Want to Know

Answers to the most common questions couples ask when planning their wedding photography.

Wedding photography in [City] typically costs between $2,500 and $6,000+ depending on hours of coverage, number of photographers, and included products. Our packages start at $2,500 for 6 hours (Essential), $3,500 for 8 hours with a second photographer (Classic), and $5,000 for full-day luxury coverage with a fine art album (Luxury).

The investment reflects far more than your wedding day alone: pre-wedding consultations, 8–12 hours of shooting, 30–40 hours of professional editing, premium camera equipment ($15,000+), insurance, and the irreplaceable expertise to capture once-in-a-lifetime moments flawlessly. View our full packages and pricing.

Most couples need 8 hours of coverage — from getting ready through to the first dances. Here's a quick guide by wedding type:

  • 4–6 hours — Elopements, micro-weddings, and ceremony-only events
  • 8 hours — Standard wedding with getting ready, ceremony, portraits, and first dances
  • 10+ hours — Large weddings, multi-venue days, or late-night receptions

We always recommend adding a 30-minute buffer on each end to handle any timing changes. We'll map your exact timeline together during your planning consultation.

Book your photographer 12–18 months in advance, especially for Saturday dates during peak season (May–October). Popular photographers book up 1–2 years ahead for sought-after dates.

The rule of thumb: book your photographer immediately after securing your venue — they're typically the first vendor to become unavailable. Off-season dates (November–March) or weekday weddings have more flexibility, but early booking is always advisable to guarantee your preferred photographer.

You'll receive a sneak peek of 20–30 edited images within 48–72 hours of your wedding — perfect for sharing on social media and with excited family.

Your full gallery of 500–800+ professionally edited images is delivered within 4–8 weeks via a private, password-protected online gallery with high-resolution download access and a full print release. Rush delivery is available on request.

A second photographer is highly recommended for weddings with 100+ guests, when the couple gets ready at different locations, or for large multi-event celebrations. They capture simultaneous moments that one photographer physically cannot — candid guest reactions while the lead captures the couple, wide ceremony overviews alongside close-up details.

Our Classic and Luxury packages include a second photographer. If you're unsure, we'll help you decide during your planning consultation based on your specific venue, timeline, and guest count.

Documentary (photojournalistic) wedding photography captures events as they naturally unfold — candid emotions, genuine reactions, and real unscripted moments without any posing or direction. The photos feel alive and authentic.

Traditional wedding photography involves more posed portraits and formal group shots — structured, timeless, and ideal for family portraits. Most couples prefer a blend of both: documentary coverage throughout the day for authentic storytelling, with a dedicated 20–30 minutes for creative couple portraits at golden hour. We tailor the balance entirely to your personality and preferences.

You can expect 75–100 professionally edited photos per hour of coverage. For an 8-hour wedding, that means 600–800 beautiful images. We prioritise quality over quantity — every delivered photo has been carefully selected from thousands of frames, professionally colour-graded, and edited.

All images are delivered as high-resolution JPEG files suitable for large-format print, alongside a full print release so you can print and share your photos anywhere, forever.

Professional wedding photography represents far more than the hours you see on your day. Each wedding involves:

  • 2–3 pre-wedding planning meetings and timeline coordination
  • 8–12 hours of wedding day shooting
  • 30–40 hours of culling thousands of raw images and professional editing
  • Professional camera bodies, lenses, and lighting ($15,000–$20,000+ equipment)
  • Backup cameras and storage for complete reliability
  • Professional liability insurance and public liability coverage
  • Gallery delivery software, cloud storage, and business costs
  • Years of specialised expertise — knowing exactly when and where to be

Most importantly, your wedding photos are a once-in-a-lifetime, irreplaceable investment. There are no retakes. A professional wedding photographer ensures your memories are captured flawlessly, even when lighting is challenging, timelines run late, or the unexpected happens.
